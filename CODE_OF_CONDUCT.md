# Code of conduct

We want J2K to be a project where anyone can report a bug, ask a question, or
send a patch and get a respectful, technical response.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Explain the evidence and trade-offs behind
  your position.
- Respect that contributors have different levels of experience and work in
  different contexts.
- Keep reviews about the code, documentation, tests, and project scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or reviews.

## Reporting

We don't have a dedicated private channel for conduct reports yet. Open a
[short issue](https://github.com/frames-sg/j2k/issues/new) asking for a private
maintainer contact, without incident details or personal information.
Maintainers will look into every report, share details only with people who
need them, and may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation
to protect the community.

Security vulnerabilities are handled separately, as described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
